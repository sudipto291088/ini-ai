# CRISP-DM response review issues

- IA does not clearly introduce the full name: **Cross-Industry Standard Process for Data Mining (CRISP-DM)**.
- IA makes unsupported generalizations, such as feature engineering being "usually the largest time sink."
- "Causal signals" overstates what CRISP-DM can establish.
- Generic topic classification fails, producing "Learning inquiry" and "Not yet classified."
- IA and Knowledge Structure profiles contradict each other.
- Prerequisites are missing in IA but overstated in the detailed Topic Profile.
- Accuracy safeguards rely on hard-coded topics instead of general validation.
- Introduction lists break sentences into grammatically incorrect bullets.
- Knowledge Map headings can be incomplete or unclear, such as "Industrial vs. customer."
- "ASUM-DM" is incorrectly named and presented as a CRISP-DM variant; IBM calls it **ASUM**.
- Modern MLOps practices are presented as though they are core CRISP-DM components.
